## Description
dailyfold is a clean, focused reading app. Instead of an endless wall of headlines, you get one full-screen story at a time, pulled from hundreds of trusted publishers and refreshed all day.

SWIPE THROUGH YOUR DAY
Scroll vertically through beautiful full-screen story cards. See the headline, image and a quick summary at a glance, then open the full article when something catches your eye. Pull down to refresh and new stories are ready and waiting.

A FEED THAT LEARNS YOU
Like the stories you enjoy and dailyfold gets better at finding more of them. The For You tab is built from your reading and the interests you choose: topics, sources, sports teams, or any custom keyword you can think of, even niche ones like bicycle repair.

THE BEST OF THE WEB, IN ONE PLACE
Read from major newsrooms, tech and science publications, health and wellness writers, and design, culture and style magazines. Turn sources on or off so your feed only includes the voices you want.

WATCH TOO
The Videos tab collects video alongside your reading, so you can read and watch without bouncing between apps.

FOLLOW THE BIG MOMENTS
Dedicated feeds for the FIFA World Cup and the Tour de France keep every story, match and stage in one place, with optional notifications so you never miss a kickoff.

YOUR READING LIST
Save articles to your Reading tab to come back to later, and share stories with friends through the native share sheet.

YOU'RE IN CONTROL
Not interested in something? Tell dailyfold and see less of it. Notifications are optional and fully adjustable. Create an account to keep your reading taste synced across sessions, and delete it any time from your profile.

dailyfold links back to the original publisher on every story. Articles are the property of their respective publishers.
